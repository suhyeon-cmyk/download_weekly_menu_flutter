# Privacy Policy for 정부청사 식단표 (Government Cafeteria Menu)

Last updated: July 6, 2026

**suhyeon-cmyk** ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **정부청사 식단표 (Government Cafeteria Menu)** mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

---

## 1. Introduction
Our primary goal is to provide a simple, secure, and privacy-respecting service. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This App is designed as a utility to download and view weekly cafeteria menus, and we do not collect, store, or share any of your personal information.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use
**We do not collect any personal data.**
- **No Account Registration:** You do not need to create an account, register, or sign in to use the App.
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect, request, or store any personal identification information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contact list.

---

## 3. Log Data & Network Permissions
The App requires the Internet permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`) solely to crawl and fetch weekly menu schedules from official cafeteria websites.
- **No Data Harvesting:** The App itself does not collect, log, or transmit any logs or device information.
- **Server Communication:** When the App fetches menu data, your request is sent directly to the destination websites. During this communication, your IP address or basic network data may be temporarily visible to the target servers in their standard network logs. We do not control, store, or access these logs.

---

## 4. Cookies
Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. 
- This App **does not use cookies** or any local tracking technologies to track your activity.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party SDKs, services, or tools that collect your data.
- **No Ads:** The App does not contain advertisement networks (such as Google AdMob).
- **No Analytics:** The App does not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services (such as Firebase Analytics, Flurry, etc.).

---

## 6. Security
We value your trust. Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers to secure or protect. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure and reliable.

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can verify and ensure no data was collected.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
